Company Overview

A Nigerian construction company with enterprise delivery discipline.

B3 Construction was established to design, construct, commission, and maintain major building facilities in Nigeria.

The company has grown from focused housing delivery into a broader engineering practice, supported by in-house expertise, continuous research, local project teams, controlled subcontractor networks, and a clear quality assurance policy.

Its operating philosophy is centered on single-point project responsibility. Clients work through a defined project manager who coordinates design, procurement, construction, safety, commissioning, and post-completion support.

HSE & Quality Assurance

Safety and quality are treated as project controls, not presentation language.

B3's company profile emphasizes risk assessment, safe working methods, procurement documentation, inspection, commissioning discipline, and public health consideration before, during, and after project delivery.

Health, Safety and Environment

Project planning considers workers, operational personnel, surrounding communities, site hazards, access, mobilization, signage, protective equipment, and safe demobilization.

  • Pre-award site visits inform early risk assessment and mobilization planning.
  • Site safety induction and orientation are conducted before workforce acceptance.
  • Final sites are left clean, safe, and fit for purpose after demobilization.

Quality Assurance and Control

B3 applies controlled documentation across procurement, project planning, inspection, supplier communication, delivery records, drawings, and client reporting.

  • Supplier certificates, calibration documents, test records, and inspection documentation are requested where applicable.
  • Project schedules and revisions are treated as controlled documents.
  • Completion reports provide formal project closure and proof of fit-for-purpose delivery.
Process

A controlled path from feasibility to post-completion support.

The delivery model is practical and procedural, built around early site intelligence, project execution planning, resource control, commissioning, and operational support.

01 / Pre-Award

Site Investigation

Review access, existing conditions, safety issues, technical constraints, mobilization needs, and feasibility factors.

02 / Award

Execution Plan

Define scope, programme, project personnel, responsibilities, method statements, drawings, and control proposals.

03 / Delivery

Mobilization & Construction

Coordinate personnel, equipment, materials, procurement, site offices, safety gear, inspections, and records.

04 / Close-Out

Commissioning & Support

Train operational staff, demobilize safely, submit completion reports, and provide technical support after handover.
